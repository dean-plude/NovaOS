| `digitest` | USB touch screens, pens and precision touchpads through the HID class driver (the devices suite's "touch" boot, 64-bit): a USB digitizer (`tools/padpeer.py --kind digitizer`) whose touch screen, pen, touchpad and configuration collections run on `hiddigi` and whose mouse collection runs on `mouhid`, under `HidUsb`; Raw Input lists the digitizer collections as HID devices and `GetPointerDevices` a pen; a touch gives `WM_POINTERDOWN`/`UP` (`PT_TOUCH`) where it touched, the pen `PT_PEN` with its pressure (`GetPointerPenInfo`), a finger on the touchpad (switched to touchpad mode at boot) moves the pointer, a tap clicks, two fingers scroll (`WM_MOUSEWHEEL`), and unplugging takes the collections and the pen away |
