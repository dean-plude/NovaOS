| `edutest` | A Windows Plug and Play driver, `edutest.sys` (`tools/drivertest/edutest.c` and `edutest.inf`, built by `tools/drivertest/mksys.py`), for QEMU's `edu` PCI device, which the devices suite's "edu" boot installs with `pnputil /add-driver C:\Tests\Edu\edutest.inf /install` once test signing is on: the driver's device interface is listed by `CM_Get_Device_Interface_List` and opens, the driver reports what `IRP_MN_START_DEVICE` gave it (edu's identification and liveness registers through the mapped 1 MiB BAR, one memory and one level-triggered interrupt resource, the vector's IRQL, a line-based connection, a version 3 DMA adapter), the device computes 10! and 12! with an interrupt each (the ISR at the interrupt's IRQL, its DPC at `DISPATCH_LEVEL`), and 4096 and 1500 bytes go to the device through a scatter/gather list and back into a common buffer and through map registers, every address within edu's 28 bits; `edutest veto` finds disabling the device refused while it is open (`CR_REMOVE_VETOED`), and `edutest absent`, after `pnputil /disable-device`, finds the interface gone |
