## GIMP opens a picture

Roadmap step 27.1 (grow the corpus), its fourth app: GIMP 2.10.38, the
image editor the App Store lists, a 64-bit GTK 2 program whose Inno Setup
installer carries 32-bit, 64-bit and ARM builds.  The nightly corpus now
runs the Store's download (put in `C:\Downloads`) silently into
`C:\Programs\GIMP 2` and opens `C:\Apps\data\picture.png`, colour bars
over a grey ramp the corpus makes (`tests/appcorpus/957-gimp.py`).  On its
first start GIMP registers about 130 plug-ins, each a program it starts
and talks to over a pair of pipes; its PNG loader is one of them, so
opening the picture needs all of that to work.  The image window must then
match `tests/reference/gimp.png`.  It needed these NovaOS fixes:

- **Missing imports.**  `kernel32`'s `GetBinaryTypeA` (GIMP asks whether a
  plug-in is 32- or 64-bit), `rpcrt4`'s `I_RpcExceptionFilter`, `mscms`'s
  `WcsGetUsePerUserProfiles`, `WcsGetDefaultColorProfileSize`,
  `WcsGetDefaultColorProfile` and `GetStandardColorSpaceProfile` (no
  monitor profile: GIMP uses sRGB), `ncrypt.dll`'s `BCrypt*` functions
  (forwarded to `bcrypt.dll`, as on Windows; GnuTLS takes its random
  numbers from there), `advapi32`'s `CryptSetProvParam`, and `msvcrt`'s
  `_snprintf_s`, `sscanf_s` and `_mbstrlen`.
- **The DLL directory is inherited.**  GIMP calls `SetDllDirectory` with its
  `bin` folder so that the plug-ins it starts from `lib\gimp\2.0\plug-ins`
  find GIMP's DLLs; on Windows a new process inherits that directory, on
  NovaOS it did not, and every plug-in failed to start.
- **ucrtbase carries the C++ runtime's helpers.**  GIMP ships Microsoft's
  `dbghelp.dll` for its crash handler, which imports `__CxxFrameHandler3`,
  `_CxxThrowException`, `__std_type_info_destroy_list` and the like from
  `api-ms-win-crt-private` (`ucrtbase.dll`); ucrtbase now forwards them to
  `vcruntime140.dll`, and gains `_wctime32`/`_wctime64`, `wmemcpy_s`,
  `wmemmove_s` and `__unDName` (which leaves the name as it is).
- **C runtime descriptors reach the child.**  GLib starts a plug-in with
  `_spawnv` and hands it its pipes by descriptor number (`-gimp 9 8`).
  Windows' C runtime passes the descriptors a child inherits in
  `STARTUPINFO`'s `lpReserved2` (a count, a flags byte each, then the
  handles) and the child's C runtime takes them up at start; NovaOS's
  `msvcrt` now does both.  And `_spawn` now joins its arguments as they
  are, as Windows' does (GLib quotes them itself; quoting them again broke
  the Python plug-ins' script paths).  `rttest` checks all three in a
  child it starts.

Under QEMU without KVM the installer finishes in about 6 minutes and GIMP
shows the picture about 20 minutes after it starts, almost all of it the
first start's plug-in registration.
