| `hidclasstest` | A HID collection the kernel's HID class driver runs (the devices suite's "hid" boot, a vendor-defined USB HID device on NovaOS's USB HID minidriver): setupapi's and `CM_Get_Device_Interface_List`'s HID interfaces, `HidD_GetAttributes`, the manufacturer, product, serial number and indexed strings, `HidD_GetPreparsedData` and `HidP_GetCaps`, `IOCTL_HID_GET_COLLECTION_INFORMATION` through `DeviceIoControl`, `HidD_SetFeature` and `GetFeature`, output reports by `WriteFile` and `HidD_SetOutputReport` read back by `ReadFile` on two handles, `HidD_GetInputReport`, `HidD_Get`/`SetNumInputBuffers`, `HidD_FlushQueue`; Raw Input: the collection in `GetRawInputDeviceList` under its interface path, `RIDI_DEVICEINFO` and `RIDI_PREPARSEDDATA`, `WM_INPUT_DEVICE_CHANGE` and an echoed report as `WM_INPUT` (x86).  `hidclasstest unplug` (64-bit) then has the test unplug the device: a read fails, the interface leaves the lists, and Raw Input sends `GIDC_REMOVAL` and drops the device |
